1 Introduction

Counting words sounds simple. It is simple, if you know your tools.

1.1 Why count words

  • Essays often have a word limit.
  • Editors pay by the word.
  • It is fun.

2 How to do it

  1. Save your text as a plain text file.
  2. Open a terminal.
  3. Run one of the commands below.
Tool Command Notes
wc wc -w file.txt fast
Pythonpython3 count.pyflexible

2.1 A small program

import sys

words = open(sys.argv[1]).read().split()
print(len(words), "words")
if len(words) < 100:
    print("a short text & a quick read")
