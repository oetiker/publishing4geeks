\section{Gauss}\label{sec:gauss}
% $...$ is maths inside the text line.
The area under the curve $e^{-x^2}$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gauss}
  \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
\end{equation}
and the young Gauss added up
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sum}
  \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}.
\end{equation}

% \label marks a place, \ref prints its number.
Equation~\ref{eq:sum} in Section~\ref{sec:gauss}
is easier than equation~\ref{eq:gauss}.
